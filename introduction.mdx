V-Extended is a fan-made Polymod modpack for Friday Night Funkin' (V-Slice engine) crafted by misterfnflover787 and MFF Studio. It adds a brand-new **V-Extended** page directly inside FNF's built-in Options menu, so you can fine-tune your gameplay feel, HUD layout, score display, and pause-menu behavior without installing any external tools or modifying game files by hand. Whether you are a rhythm-game veteran chasing perfect accuracy or a casual player who just wants a cleaner HUD, V-Extended gives you the knobs and toggles to make FNF truly yours.

## Choose Your Version

V-Extended comes in two flavors — one for PC players and one for mobile players. Both share a core set of options, and each adds extras that make sense for its platform. Pick the tab below that matches how you play.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="V-Extended (PC)">
    **Version:** 1.4.5 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Platform:** PC (Windows / Linux / macOS)

    The PC release is the full-featured version of V-Extended. In addition to all shared options, it includes two gameplay tweaks that are especially useful on keyboard and controller:

    - **Ghost Tapping** — Press note keys freely without missing when no notes are on screen, so stray inputs never tank your combo.
    - **Middlescroll** — Shifts your strumline to the horizontal center of the screen, letting you focus on a smaller area during intense charts.

    **All shared options are also available on PC:**

    - Options in Pause Menu
    - Botplay in Pause
    - Opponent Notes Opacity
    - Strumline Background
    - Time Bar
    - Detailed Score Counter
    - Score Counter Outline
    - Accuracy Precision
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="V-Extended Mobile">
    **Version:** 1.2.1 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Platform:** Mobile (Android / iOS)

    V-Extended Mobile is purpose-built for touch screens. Alongside all the shared options, it brings two mobile-specific additions unavailable on PC:

    - **Alternative Note Input** — Replaces the default mobile controls with a full-screen, four-lane touch layout, so every lane maps to a large tap zone that is easy to hit on the go.
    - **Strumline Background Padding** — Controls how far the lane background panels extend beyond the arrows, giving you extra visual breathing room on smaller screens.

    **All shared options are also available on Mobile:**

    - Options in Pause Menu
    - Botplay in Pause
    - Opponent Notes Opacity
    - Strumline Background
    - Time Bar
    - Detailed Score Counter
    - Score Counter Outline
    - Accuracy Precision
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Shared Options at a Glance

Both variants give you the same core set of customization options inside the **V-Extended** section of the Options menu.

| Option | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Options in Pause Menu** | Adds a shortcut to the Options menu directly from the pause screen. |
| **Botplay in Pause** | Lets you toggle Botplay on or off while a song is paused. |
| **Opponent Notes Opacity** | Controls how visible the opponent's strumline notes are during gameplay. |
| **Strumline Background** | Adds a background panel behind the strumline for improved readability. |
| **Time Bar** | Displays a progress bar showing how far through the current song you are. |
| **Detailed Score Counter** | Expands the score display to show hit judgements and combo information. |
| **Score Counter Outline** | Adds an outline to the score text so it stands out against any background. |
| **Accuracy Precision** | Adjusts how many decimal places are shown in your accuracy percentage. |

<Tip>
  Not sure where to start? Enable **Detailed Score Counter** and **Accuracy Precision** first — seeing your exact performance numbers is a great way to spot which parts of a chart need the most practice.
</Tip>
