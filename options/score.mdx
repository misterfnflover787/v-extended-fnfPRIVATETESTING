V-Extended's Detailed Score Counter replaces FNF's default score display with a richer readout that shows your current score, miss count, accuracy percentage, and a Freeplay-style rank letter — all visible during play. You can further customize the look with an optional outline and control exactly how many decimal places appear on your accuracy percentage.

<ParamField body="Detailed Score Counter" type="checkbox" default="enabled">
  Replaces the standard score text with score, misses, accuracy, and a Freeplay-style rank letter. When enabled, the full detailed readout is shown on the HUD during gameplay. When disabled, the vanilla FNF score display is used instead.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Score Counter Outline" type="checkbox" default="enabled">
  Adds a black outline around every part of the detailed score counter, improving legibility against bright or busy backgrounds.
</ParamField>

<Note>
  Score Counter Outline only has a visible effect when **Detailed Score Counter** is enabled. If the detailed counter is off, toggling the outline has no impact on the display.
</Note>

<ParamField body="Score Outline Size" type="number (1–4 px, step 1)" default="2 px">
  Changes the thickness of the score counter outline. Higher values produce a bolder, more prominent outline.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Accuracy Precision" type="enum" default="2 Decimals">
  Selects how many decimal places are shown for your accuracy percentage. Choose from three levels of detail:

  - **No Decimals** — Displays accuracy as a whole number (e.g., `98%`). Best for a clean, minimal readout.
  - **1 Decimal** — Displays one decimal place (e.g., `98.6%`). A balanced middle ground.
  - **2 Decimals** — Displays two decimal places (e.g., `98.63%`). The most precise readout available.
</ParamField>

<Tip>
  If you're going for high-accuracy runs, setting Accuracy Precision to **2 Decimals** gives you the most precise readout and makes it easier to spot even small improvements between attempts.
</Tip>
