Welcome to V-Extended, the fan-made Polymod modpack that takes your Friday Night Funkin' V-Slice experience to the next level. Created by MFF Studio, V-Extended slots a dedicated options page right into FNF's built-in Options menu, giving you hands-on control over gameplay feel, HUD layout, score display, and pause-menu shortcuts — all without ever leaving the game. Whether you play on PC or mobile, V-Extended has a variant built specifically for your platform.

## Get Up and Running in Three Steps

Getting V-Extended into your game is quick and straightforward. Follow these three steps and you will be customizing your FNF session in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download V-Extended">
    Grab the correct release for your platform — **V-Extended v1.4.5** for PC or **V-Extended Mobile v1.2.1** for mobile — from the official MFF Studio release page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Modpack">
    Drop the downloaded Polymod package into your FNF `mods` folder (PC) or follow the mobile sideload instructions, then launch the game to let Polymod register the mod automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Options">
    Open the in-game **Options** menu and navigate to the new **V-Extended** section. Toggle and adjust each setting to match your playstyle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  V-Extended and V-Extended Mobile are **separate downloads** built for their respective platforms. Make sure you grab the right package — installing the PC version on a mobile build (or vice versa) will not work correctly.
</Note>
